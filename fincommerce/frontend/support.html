Frequently Asked Questions

How do refunds work in FinCommerce?

Once a return request is approved, the full refund amount is instantly credited to your digital wallet balance.

Is my card or bank information stored?

No! All raw card numbers, CVVs, and sensitive credentials are masked or handled via secure mock gateways.

What are the active coupon codes?

Try using WELCOME10 (10% OFF) or SAVE500 (₹500 OFF on orders above ₹5,000).
